# Story 2.4 — Graduation retake
Points: 5 | Tool: opsx:plan + opsx:apply | ADRs: 013

Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A GRADUATED stage can be retaken at any time for confidence.
- [ ] A retake that fails does not revoke GRADUATED status.
- [ ] Every retake, pass or fail, is stored as its own graduation_attempt row.
- [ ] The stage map shows best result and latest result separately.
